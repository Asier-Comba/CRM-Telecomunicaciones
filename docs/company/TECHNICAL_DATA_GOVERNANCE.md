# Technical company and tenant governance

Company ownership applies to GitHub, hosting, DNS, mail, Supabase, offsite, monitoring and optional automation/AI accounts. Maintain named users, minimum permissions, billing/asset/security owners, two recoverable MFA administrators, and an individual offboarding checklist. Environment records include provider account, project, region, exact origin, image/config/migration digests and secret references. No company name is hardcoded into runtime deployment.

Data-flow inventory: browser authenticates through Auth/cookie transport; current database membership and tenant-bound RPCs authorize CRM data; private Storage uses authorized tickets; server provider secrets stay outside client assets. Auth SMTP handles account messages. The future CRM outbox requires current actor/tenant authority, approved templates, consent, allowlisted staging recipients and idempotent receipts. n8n and W3 remain separately gated; neither receives arbitrary CRM payloads from this preparatory platform. Backups contain sensitive data and require AEAD, protected keys, separate-account storage, retention and authenticated readback. Monitoring uses aggregate metadata only.

For each selected provider record contracting entity, service/data categories, subprocessor list, region/residence, transfer mechanism if applicable, retention, export/deletion capability, breach contact and evidence owner. Legal owners must determine obligations and approve agreements. This technical inventory is not a GDPR certification or legal opinion.

Tenant onboarding uses the existing authorized backend: company administrator establishes the workspace and membership, checks tenant isolation/private buckets and assigns bounded quotas. No public signup, hidden super-admin bypass or parallel billing authority is introduced. Billing activation is a company operational decision; disable provider effects while commercial approval is absent.

Tenant deactivation: block new claims/provider effects and remove current memberships through accepted backend operations; invalidate outstanding tickets/permissions, reconcile confirmed pending effects, capture auditable metadata and obtain an authorized export. Apply approved retention/legal holds before deletion. Backup immutability can delay final physical erasure; document the policy rather than claiming instant deletion. Destructive provider deletes require their own approved procedure. Offboarding people must revoke Auth/provider/repository permissions individually and rotate shared machine secrets where required.

Capacity and cost decisions use `infra/platform/cost-policy.json`: prices and quantities remain unapproved/null. Obtain current provider quotes from owners, document currency/taxes/limits, set spend caps and measure DB connections, upload/private Storage, SSE, mail, automation and worker queues before approval. No subscriptions/purchases or production SLA are created by the baseline CPU/memory limits.
